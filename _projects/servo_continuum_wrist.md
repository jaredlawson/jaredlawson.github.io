I created this kit assignment for the Desktop Manufacturing course at Vanderbilt for junior/senior level undergraduates. This included laser-cutting notches of a multi-lumen tube to create a wire-actuated continuum wrist. This wrist was then driven by a potentiometer-driven servo motor.
